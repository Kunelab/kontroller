The app is now called KuneLab Kontroller and is part of Kunelab. It comes with a new
application ID and a newly drawn launcher icon, which also keep it clearly distinct
from the upstream Kontroller project it was forked from.

Because the application ID changed, Android treats this as a separate app: the
MaxKontroller 0.2.1 you installed from GitHub will not be replaced, and you can
remove it once you have moved over.

Reconnection is smarter: retries back off gradually, and a stale pairing (the host
accepts the link, then drops it at once) no longer causes an endless rapid retry
storm -- the app stops after a few tries and suggests re-pairing instead.

The app also repairs a wedged keyboard registration by itself after a crash, update
or force stop, instead of asking you to toggle Bluetooth off and on.
