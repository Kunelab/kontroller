Typing on a Turkish host is fixed. Three characters came out wrong because the app
sent the wrong key position: an apostrophe arrived as a slash, a slash arrived as a
full stop, and a plus arrived as an underscore. Other host layouts are unaffected.

The rest of this release is internal tidying with no change to how the app behaves:
unused code and resources removed, and the documentation corrected where it had
drifted out of date.
